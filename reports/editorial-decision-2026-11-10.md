# Editorial decision — November 10, 2026

## Search Console input

The private `LOKWOD/site-analytics` reports were generated November 4, 2026 and cover October 5 through November 1. NauticalDream.com recorded 2 clicks, 39 impressions, 5.1% CTR and average position 11.7. The HTTPS homepage held 36 impressions and both clicks. The only repeated queries were branded variants: “nautical dreams” (9 impressions, 1 click, average position 7.1) and “nautical dream” (3 impressions, 1 click, average position 11.3).

That evidence supports a current homepage lead, hub placement, reciprocal contextual links and sitemap discovery. It does not support a new query-led page. One- to three-impression fragments, malformed terms, unrelated “nautical adventures” wording and the HTTP homepage row were deliberately ignored as too weak or irrelevant. No item is labeled Data-led today.

## Complete-inventory and fourteen-day check

The pre-run inventory covered all 475 HTML titles, slugs, H1 subjects, descriptions/search intents, section angles, image associations and commercial categories, plus 512 credited image hashes. The previous fourteen days and all earlier inventory were checked. No selected topic duplicates an existing title, slug, intent, category, destination or image:

- Existing cart and paddlesports pages do not join exact hull support zones, full loaded weight, contact geometry, strap route, wheel surface and carry-route proof in one buying record.
- Existing New York paddling coverage does not provide a Lake Taghkanic guide separating its west hand launch, east unimproved trailer launch, required permit and electric-only propulsion rule.
- Existing emergency and propeller pages do not provide a property-only dropped-object decision that explicitly prevents a child or adult from entering the water.

## Diverse portfolio

1. **Authority-expansion — Kayak and Canoe Cart Fit Guide.** High-intent category guidance with three tightly relevant commercial links and a ten-field fit record.
2. **Exploration — Lake Taghkanic Family Paddling Guide.** A distinct Hudson Valley destination with an eight-gate launch, permit, weather and return card.
3. **Authority-expansion — Dropped Gear Overboard Decision Guide.** A noncommercial family-seamanship framework that keeps property recovery separate from person overboard.

The trio differs in intent, reader problem, format and primary subject: buy compatible transport gear, plan a specific family trip, and prevent an impulsive recovery from becoming an emergency.

## Primary-source and safety boundaries

- Cart facts use RAILBLAZA and Malone manuals/specification pages; published figures are attributed to the named families and never generalized.
- Lake Taghkanic facts use the current New York State Parks page, final master-plan appendices and official launch booklet; volatile fees and services are check-before-you-go items.
- Dropped-object guidance uses Coast Guard Navigation Rules, federal equipment and engine-cut-off guidance, plus NOAA debris handling. The sequence is explicitly conservative editorial planning, not an official maneuver.
