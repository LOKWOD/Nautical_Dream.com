The HTTPS homepage accounted for 50 impressions, 1 click, 2.0% CTR and average position 6.6. The only repeated query evidence was branded: “nautical dream” had 21 impressions at position 6.7, while “nautical dreams,” “nautical dri” and the single “.com” click were too small or irrelevant to steer a page.

Decision: no page is labeled Data-led. The credible signal calls for a clearer homepage lead and stronger hub/contextual discovery, so the three new decisions are surfaced directly and six established pages link back into them. Tiny branded and malformed samples were deliberately ignored rather than turned into keyword variants.

## Inventory and duplication gate

The review covered all 367 pre-publication root HTML files, every publication source module, the editorial catalog, sitemap, RSS, affiliate configuration/report, image attribution manifest, hubs, prior fourteen days and all existing title/canonical records. The selected slugs, subjects, intents and image concepts are distinct:

- the Raquette Lake page is specifically for Golden Beach's hand launch and portable small boats, not another trailer-ramp lake guide;
- the boatyard page governs custody, scope and evidence, not the mechanical winterization procedure or a generic marina-contract list.

## Authority and trust work

Three citation-worthy assets were created, six established pages gained reciprocal contextual links and all three new pages link into relevant cleaning, Adirondack and seasonal-ownership clusters. Technical boundaries direct model-specific procedures to manuals and qualified technicians; the destination guide distinguishes a hand launch from a trailer ramp; image captions clearly separate conceptual scenes from documentary photography. No outreach opportunities were logged because no tightly matched target improved on the primary-source cluster established today.
