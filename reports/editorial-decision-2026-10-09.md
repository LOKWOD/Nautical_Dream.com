## Inventory and collision check

The pre-publication inventory covered 379 HTML pages, 39 dated content modules with 102 daily source items, and 320 image-attribution records. Titles, slugs, core subjects, search intent, product categories, destinations, internal targets and visual identities were compared across the complete site; the previous fourteen days were reviewed separately.

The selected questions were absent: selecting among three emergency cutting-tool geometries, matching a family boat to Salmon River Reservoir's four official access types, and calculating a shoulder-season return deadline from five independent clocks. Broad cold-water primers, generic boat tools, recent dockless-launch coverage and nearby recently published lake guides were rejected as collisions.
