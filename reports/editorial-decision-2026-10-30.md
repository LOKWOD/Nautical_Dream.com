# Editorial decision — October 30, 2026

## Search Console input

The connected report generated October 28, 2026 covers September 28 through October 25. NauticalDream.com recorded 40 impressions, two clicks, a 5.0% CTR and average position 12.4. The HTTPS homepage produced 37 impressions and both clicks. The only repeated position-4–20 phrase was branded “nautical dreams” with 16 impressions at average position 7.6 and no clicks. That pattern supports clearer homepage-to-hub discovery, not another branded or query-variant article. One-to-three-impression fragments, the three-impression HTTP-homepage row and malformed or unrelated terms were deliberately ignored as too weak or irrelevant.

## Portfolio decision

- **Authority-expansion:** Boat windshield-wiper fit. A high-intent equipment guide resolves five interfaces—electrical, shaft, park, sweep and arm/blade—without claiming product tests or universal fit.
- **Exploration:** Buffalo Harbor State Park with a family boat. This expands the destination library into a Western New York urban Great Lakes launch and uses current State Parks and NWS gates rather than repeating the recent Adirondack small-ramp cluster.
- **Authority-expansion:** A sensory-aware family boat day. This serves an inclusive seamanship need with a nonmedical communication matrix, hearing-protection boundary and short-return rule.

No page is labeled Data-led because the available nonbranded sample is not credible enough to steer a new URL. The batch is diverse in primary subject, reader problem, intent and format: equipment compatibility and purchase, named-place trip planning, and family operating practice.

## Duplicate and cannibalization control

The complete pre-publication inventory and the previous fourteen days were reviewed across titles, slugs, H1 subjects, descriptions/search intent, section angles, image associations and commercial categories. Another trailer accessory, Adirondack access page, maintenance-record variant, branded-query page or hearing-loss duplicate was rejected. The sensory guide links to the existing hearing-loss page but has a distinct purpose: individualized sequence, sensory load and caregiver communication rather than hearing-status operations.
