# Editorial decision — October 31, 2026

## Search Console input

The connected report generated October 28, 2026 covers September 28 through October 25. NauticalDream.com recorded 40 impressions, two clicks, a 5.0% CTR and average position 12.4. The HTTPS homepage produced 37 impressions and both clicks. The only repeated position-4–20 phrase was branded “nautical dreams,” with 16 impressions at average position 7.6 and no clicks. That supports stronger homepage-to-hub crawl paths, not a new branded-query article. One-to-three-impression fragments, the three-impression HTTP-homepage row and irrelevant or unstable terms were deliberately ignored as too weak to steer a URL.
