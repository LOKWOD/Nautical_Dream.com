# Search Console editorial decisions

## 2026-11-03

- Report checked: `LOKWOD/site-analytics` generated 2026-10-28, covering 2026-09-28 through 2026-10-25.
- Site signal: 40 impressions, 2 clicks, 5% CTR and average position 12.4; the HTTPS homepage carried 37 impressions at average position 13.08.
- Query signal used: the only repeated terms were branded variants (“nautical dreams,” 16 impressions at position 7.625; “nautical dream,” 3 impressions with one click). This supported stronger homepage and hub discovery, not a near-duplicate branded article.
- Weak signal deliberately ignored: one- to three-impression queries, the malformed HTTP homepage row and unrelated “nautical adventures” / “nautical day” noise were too small or irrelevant to redirect the slate.
- Editorial effect: no page was labeled Data-led. The batch instead adds one high-intent compatibility asset, one distinct Western New York launch plan and one mixed-use-water seamanship asset, while reinforcing the homepage, three hubs, reciprocal links, sitemap, feed, catalog and `llms.txt`.
