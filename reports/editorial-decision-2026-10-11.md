# Editorial decision — October 11, 2026

## Search Console input

The connected `site-analytics` report was generated October 7, 2026 and covers September 7 through October 4. Nautical Dream recorded 3 clicks, 53 impressions, 5.7% CTR and average position 6.0. The usable pattern was branded homepage discovery: the HTTPS homepage earned 51 impressions and two clicks at average position 6.2; the queries “nautical dream” and “nautical dreams” accounted for 30 impressions without clicks.

That signal supported a stronger current homepage lead, hub cards, reciprocal cluster links, sitemap, RSS and `llms.txt` discovery. It did not justify a Data-led article because the report contained no sufficiently supported nonbranded page or query opportunity. The `.com` query (one impression and one click), two-impression HTTP homepage row and the report's inconsistent zero-indexed sitemap field were deliberately ignored as too small or contradicted by active impressions.

## Inventory and collision check

The source generators, 387 existing indexable URLs, titles, canonical slugs, article subjects, destinations, product categories, search intents, commercial anchors and image records were inventoried. The October 1–10 batches were reviewed separately. Dedicated trailer-hub inspection, Hinckley Reservoir access and post-storm winter-cover inspection pages were absent.

Generic trailer accessories, general winterization, storage ventilation, low-water ramp advice and nearby reservoir pages were rejected as collisions. The selected pages solve narrower problems: exact hub identity and roadside comparison, two distinct Hinckley access types, and a ground-level storm inspection that does not teach snow removal.

## Portfolio

- **Authority-expansion:** Boat trailer wheel bearings — high-intent maintenance and buying guidance built around an axle-specific identity card, repeatable hub comparison and exact-spare gate.
- **Exploration:** Hinckley Reservoir — a new Central New York destination guide that separates NYPA's conditional trailered ramp from DEC's seasonal small-craft beach.
- **Authority-expansion:** Stored boat cover after snow or wind — a seven-zone perimeter and escalation protocol connecting weather, storage and cover-maker guidance.

The pages differ in reader problem, format, subject and intent: road-system maintenance, named-waterbody trip planning and winter storage risk management. No item is Data-led because branded homepage impressions are a navigation signal, not evidence for a new query-family page.

## Authority and trust work

Three citation-ready assets were created: the hub identity card, the Hinckley two-access/water-level gate and the seven-zone post-storm perimeter card. Six established pages received reciprocal contextual links, strengthening trailer systems, Central New York destinations and winter-storage clusters. Each page distinguishes universal caution from manufacturer- or facility-specific instructions, uses current primary sources and names the qualified-professional boundary.

No outreach was sent. No external opportunity was logged because the source agencies are better treated as fact-check and update contacts than as prospective endorsers of an affiliate publication; the authority action for this run is the improved site-owned reference material and cluster structure.
