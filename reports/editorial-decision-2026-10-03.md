## Portfolio

- **Data-led — Boat First-Aid Kits: Buy the Base, Build the Boat Layer, Keep It Reachable.** Uses the only supported page signal—the homepage's impressions and weak CTR—to provide a clear high-intent safety entry point. Its five-layer fit card distinguishes published baseline contents from crew, trip, protection and access needs.
- **Authority-expansion — Conesus Lake by Boat: A Family Day Built Around the State Launch.** Expands the destination cluster into Western New York with a current State Parks launch record, live 2026 dock notice, no-power-loading rule and source-dated launch-day matrix.
- **Exploration — Boat Cooler Food Safety: Pack Cold, Measure It, and Know What to Throw Out.** Adds an underserved family health/logistics branch using USDA's boating-specific guidance, FDA outdoor-food rules and a measurable cooler-control card.

The mix is deliberately diverse: a commercial safety-equipment decision, a place-based family operating plan and a public-health protocol. They differ in search intent, subject, format and primary reader problem.

## Authority and trust work

Created three reusable citation-ready assets: the first-aid five-layer fit card, Conesus launch-day matrix and boat-food control card. Added six reciprocal contextual links from established pages and direct primary-source notes that separate official facts from editorial inference. The Conesus image is explicitly conceptual rather than documentary; the product images are generic category visuals and make no model or testing claim.

No outreach opportunities were logged. The authority action is the site-owned decision material, the new Western Finger Lakes destination branch and stronger first-aid and family-provisioning clusters.
