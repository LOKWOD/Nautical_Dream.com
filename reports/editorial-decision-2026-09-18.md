## Collision review

The complete HTML inventory and the previous fourteen days of source publications were checked for titles, slugs, subject terms, internal-link targets and image keys. Existing battery pages cover chemistry, sizing, switching, charging, securement and maintenance but not monitor architecture and calibration. Existing canal coverage is system-wide or centered on other waterways; no Little Falls trailer-day itinerary exists. Existing failure pages cover engine stall and grounding but do not distinguish proposed routine assistance from possible salvage terms.

## Authority action

The tow-versus-salvage page adds a citation-ready assistance-classification card that separates distress, routine towing and potentially specialized recovery without pretending to decide maritime law. Reciprocal contextual links connect it to the engine-stall and grounding protocols. The Little Falls guide adds a current official-source decision card and a reciprocal Erie Canal link. The battery-maintenance guide now points to the monitor-selection matrix.

No outreach was sent or logged. The authority work improves the site's safety, ownership and canal topic clusters without depending on a backlink.
