## Search Console input

The private analytics report was generated **2026-09-30** for the window ending **2026-09-27**. NauticalDream.com recorded 2 clicks, 58 impressions, 3.4% CTR and average position 6.1. The HTTPS homepage accounted for 50 impressions, 1 click, 2.0% CTR and average position 6.6. Query evidence was almost entirely branded (for example, “nautical dream” with 21 impressions), while the single “.com” click and very small variants were too weak or irrelevant to direct an article.

Decision: no new page is labeled Data-led. The credible signal supports stronger homepage discovery and clearer topic breadth, so the homepage lead now presents the three new decisions directly. Tiny branded and irrelevant samples were deliberately ignored rather than turned into speculative keyword pages.

## Inventory and duplication gate

The review covered all 364 existing root HTML files, all publication source modules, the complete sitemap and RSS catalog, title/canonical records, affiliate rules, image attribution records, hubs, the previous fourteen days of publication sources, and the Wednesday-independent destination inventory. - the footwear page is a fit-and-use comparison, not another PFD, boarding-ladder or packing list;

- the reset page handles the first fifteen post-trip minutes and does not replace the detailed cleaning or maintenance schedules.
