# Editorial decision — November 2, 2026

## Search Console input

The connected `LOKWOD/site-analytics` report was generated October 28, 2026 and covers September 28 through October 25. NauticalDream.com recorded 40 impressions, 2 clicks, 5.0% CTR and average position 12.4. The only repeated position-4–20 query was the branded **“nautical dreams”** query (16 impressions, position 7.6); the HTTPS homepage carried nearly all meaningful page impressions.

That evidence supports a fresh homepage lead, strong hub cards and reciprocal crawl paths. It does not support a branded article or a second URL competing for the homepage’s intent. One-to-three-impression fragments, the malformed HTTP homepage row and loosely related phrases were deliberately ignored as too weak, unstable or irrelevant. No page was forced into the Data-led label.

## Inventory and rejection gate

The November 1 complete-site inventory covered 451 HTML pages and 464 credited image associations, with complete hashes for titles, slugs, H1 subjects, descriptions/search intent, section angles, commercial categories and image associations. The final topics have no matching title, slug, primary intent, destination or image.
