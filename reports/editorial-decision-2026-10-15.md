# Editorial decision — 2026-10-15

## Search Console signal

The `LOKWOD/site-analytics` report was generated **2026-10-14** and covers **2026-09-14 through 2026-10-11**. The `nauticaldream.com` property recorded 2 clicks, 61 impressions, 3.3% CTR and average position 9.5. The HTTPS homepage carried 56 impressions, one click, 1.8% CTR and average position 10.1. Repeat branded queries—“nautical dream” and “nautical dreams”—accounted for 33 impressions at average positions 7.7–7.9.

That homepage/branded pattern is sufficiently coherent to justify stronger homepage, hub and reciprocal discovery, but it is not a sound basis for three same-family articles. No new page is labeled Data-led today because the nonbranded query rows are isolated one-impression samples at weak positions. The `.com` fragment, HTTP-home row and single-impression “nautical adventures” / “nautical day” rows were deliberately ignored as too small, navigational or irrelevant to redirect the editorial plan.
