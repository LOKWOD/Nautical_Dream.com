Each page identifies current primary sources, dates volatile access research, distinguishes general rules from product- or site-specific instructions and makes the AI-assisted image status explicit.

No outreach opportunities were logged. The authority action is the improved site-owned reference material and stronger fuel, Central New York access and family-safety topic clusters.
