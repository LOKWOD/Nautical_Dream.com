# Editorial decision — 2026-10-10

## Search Console input

The private `LOKWOD/site-analytics` report was generated 2026-10-07 and covers 2026-09-07 through 2026-10-04. NauticalDream.com recorded 3 clicks, 53 impressions, 5.7% CTR and average position 6.0. The HTTPS homepage accounted for 51 impressions and 2 clicks (3.9% CTR, position 6.2). The visible queries were branded or fragmentary: “nautical dream” (17 impressions), “nautical dreams” (13), “nautical dri” (5), and “.com” (1).

No nonbranded query or article URL had enough evidence for a responsible data-led page. The branded homepage signal therefore influenced discovery rather than topic repetition: all three pages were placed in the refreshed homepage lead, their hubs, RSS, sitemap and `llms.txt`, and six established pages now link back contextually. The single `.com` impression and fragmentary queries were ignored as weak or irrelevant.

The report's sitemap row listed 260 submitted URLs, zero indexed URLs, no errors or warnings and a last download of 2026-08-17. That is stale and inconsistent with the property's current impressions, so it was not treated as literal current coverage. The batch responds conservatively by improving crawl paths instead of inventing an indexing diagnosis.

## Inventory and collision check

The pre-publication inventory covered 382 HTML pages, 40 dated publication modules with 105 daily source items, and 326 image-attribution records. Titles, slugs, subjects, search intents, product categories, destinations, internal targets and visual identities were checked across the complete site; the previous fourteen days were reviewed separately.

The selected questions were absent: choosing among three boat-flooring systems by attachment and future removal, planning a day from Stillwater Reservoir's one official hard-surface launch and wind exposure, and fitting a life jacket over the actual cold-weather layer system. Generic flooring inspiration, another nearby launch-only page and another broad cold-water primer were rejected as collisions.

## Authority and trust work

Three citation-ready assets were created: a five-gate flooring matrix with removal-cost worksheet, a Stillwater launch-and-margin card, and a six-motion cold-layer fit protocol. Six established pages received reciprocal contextual links, strengthening the maintenance/refit, Western Adirondack destination and cold-water/life-jacket clusters. Each page separates official fact, manufacturer-specific instruction and editorial inference, and each generated destination image is labeled conceptual rather than documentary.

No outreach was sent or logged. The authority action is the improved site-owned reference material, primary-source record and reciprocal topic-cluster structure.
