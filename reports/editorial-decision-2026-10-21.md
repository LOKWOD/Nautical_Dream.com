# Editorial decision — October 21, 2026

## Search Console decision

The `LOKWOD/site-analytics` report generated **October 14, 2026** was seven days old and valid for NauticalDream.com. The property showed **2 clicks, 61 impressions, 3.3% CTR and average position 9.5**. The HTTPS homepage carried 56 impressions at position 10.1, while the visible query rows were mainly low-volume branded or fragmented variants such as “nautical dream,” “nautical dreams,” “.com,” and “nautical dri.” That is not sufficient evidence for a new article intent.

Decision: no page is labeled Data-led. The credible signal was a discovery/hub opportunity, so this batch strengthens the homepage, three hubs, reciprocal article links, feed, sitemap and `llms.txt`. Tiny branded and malformed query fragments were deliberately ignored as too weak or irrelevant to steer a subject. The sitemap row’s August 17 submission date was treated as a discovery warning, not proof that no URLs are indexed.

## Diversity decision

- **Authority-expansion:** *Boat Washdown Hoses and Nozzles* adds a non-overlapping source–pressure–route–storage fit card to the boat-care cluster and serves honest category-shopping intent.
- **Exploration:** *Lake Bonaparte by Boat* adds a North Country access page built around the official 14-space launch record, deliberately avoiding claims about unlisted facilities or a fictional route.
- **Authority-expansion:** *New York Boating Safety Certificates* connects current statewide operator proof to the existing documents and guest-operator cluster without replacing the official course or certificate.

The three pages differ in subject, reader problem, format and intent: purchase compatibility, destination access, and legal/ownership planning. The first proposed gear topic—trailer tongue jacks—was rejected after the complete inventory revealed an existing September guide with the same intent.

## Inventory gate

The pre-publication site contained 415 HTML pages. Existing titles, slugs, headings, product categories, destinations, section angles, commercial anchors and credited images were checked from the full generated inventory plus the prior fourteen days of publication sources. The final three slugs, titles and primary intents did not exist before this batch.
