# Editorial decision — November 4, 2026

## Search Console input

The connected report was generated **October 28, 2026** and covers September 28–October 25. NauticalDream.com recorded **40 impressions, 2 clicks, 5.0% CTR and average position 12.4**. The only repeatable signal was branded homepage visibility: `nautical dreams` produced 16 impressions at position 7.6, while the canonical homepage produced 37 impressions and both clicks. That signal justified strengthening current homepage and hub crawl paths, not another branded page. The 1–3 impression queries (`nautical day`, `nautical adventures`, the HTTP homepage) were deliberately ignored as too weak, ambiguous or protocol noise.

## Portfolio

1. **Authority-expansion — Marine Canvas Snap Fasteners.** A high-intent compatibility guide adds a new canvas-hardware subtopic without duplicating cover buying, cleaning, storage or general repair pages. Its four-part identity record and fastener-to-tool chain are citation-worthy owner resources.
2. **Authority-expansion — Boat Repair Work Orders.** A ten-field estimate, diagnostic-limit and change-order worksheet extends boat-ownership authority into service authorization without competing with maintenance records, DIY gates or seasonal yard handoff pages.
3. **Exploration — Family Food-Allergy Boat Day Plan.** An underserved inclusive-family need adds a conservative food, medication, crew-role and exit framework distinct from cooler packing, first aid, non-swimmer and sensory-aware guides.

The batch is diverse in primary subject, format, search intent and reader problem: hardware buying compatibility, owner/vendor cost control, and family medical-logistics planning. No page is primarily data-led because the current non-branded query sample is not strong enough to justify query-cluster reinforcement.

## Duplicate review

The complete inventory and previous fourteen days were checked for titles, slugs, H1 subjects, descriptions/search intent, section angles, images and commercial categories. The rejected near-neighbor risks were steering/control cables, another launch guide, trailer hardware, another insurance checklist, cooler-only food safety and generic first-aid packing. The selected pages each create a new decision framework.
