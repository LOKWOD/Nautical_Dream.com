# Editorial decision — November 5, 2026

## Search Console input

The private `LOKWOD/site-analytics` reports were generated November 4, 2026 and cover October 5 through November 1. NauticalDream.com recorded 2 clicks, 39 impressions, 5.1% CTR and average position 11.7. The HTTPS homepage held 36 impressions and both clicks. The only repeated queries were branded variants: “nautical dreams” (9 impressions, 1 click, average position 7.1) and “nautical dream” (3 impressions, 1 click, average position 11.3).

That evidence supports stronger homepage, hub, reciprocal-link and sitemap discovery. It does not support another branded page or a narrow query-led article. One- and two-impression fragments, unrelated wording and the three-impression HTTP homepage row were deliberately ignored as too weak, malformed or irrelevant. No item is labeled Data-led today.

## Complete-inventory and fourteen-day check

The generated inventory covers every HTML title, slug, H1 subject, description/search intent, H2 angle, image association and commercial category, plus every credited image hash. The review also isolates pages published since October 22. No selected topic duplicates an existing title, slug, primary intent, product category, destination guide or image:

- No existing page provides a complete replacement-propeller purchase record joining engine/drive identity, hub architecture, diameter, pitch, rotation, thrust hardware and loaded WOT RPM.
- No existing page covers Thompson's Lake State Park's carry-in-only park access, seasonal rental gate and separate DEC trailer-launch distinction.
- No existing page builds a warranty-specific evidence file joining written coverage, identity, maintenance, symptom, authorization, recall/defect and resolution records.

## Diverse portfolio

1. **Authority-expansion — Boat Propeller Replacement Buying Guide.** High-intent compatibility guide with three tightly relevant, category-level affiliate links and a reusable eight-field fit record.
2. **Exploration — Thompson's Lake State Park Carry-In Family Paddling Guide.** A new Capital Region family destination and short proof-loop plan, materially different from trailer-ramp and motorboat guides.
3. **Authority-expansion — Boat Warranty Claim Evidence File.** A noncommercial ownership system connecting maintenance and work-order coverage with FTC and USCG primary sources.

The pages differ in subject, reader problem, format and intent: buy the right mechanical interface, plan a conservative family outing, and preserve a contractual/safety record.

## Primary-source and safety boundaries

- Propeller facts use Yamaha and Mercury owner guidance. Manufacturer application data and the exact engine/drive manual control; no universal RPM rule, performance promise or installation procedure is invented.
- Thompson's Lake facts use New York State Parks and NYSDEC. Fees, dates, rentals and amenities are marked volatile, and the park carry-in is not conflated with the separate DEC launch.
- Warranty guidance uses FTC consumer/federal warranty material, USCG recall/defect resources and a clearly labeled Mercury product-specific example. The page is not legal advice or a coverage promise.

The batch creates three reusable, citation-ready assets: an eight-field propeller fit record, a six-gate Thompson's Lake go/hold/leave card, and a six-folder warranty evidence index. Six established pages receive reciprocal contextual links, connecting the propeller, family-paddling and ownership-record clusters.
