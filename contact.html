Get in touch

Talk to the editorial desk.

Corrections, local knowledge, products, photography and thoughtful partnerships are welcome.

General and editorial inquiries

Email info@nauticaldream.com. A useful message includes a clear subject, the relevant page or product and enough detail to understand the request without a follow-up exchange.

Corrections and local knowledge

Marina policies, lock hours, fuel availability, restaurant operations and product specifications change. If a page is wrong or out of date, send the exact statement, a current primary source and the date you verified it. First-hand local observations are valuable when the location, weather and vessel context are included.

Safety corrections receive priority.

If information could create a navigation, equipment or personal-safety risk, put “Safety correction” in the subject line. Nautical Dream is not an emergency service; contact the appropriate authorities for an active incident.

Products and editorial consideration

Brands may send a concise description, exact model number, current specifications, retail availability, warranty details and high-resolution photography with usage rights. Sending a product or information does not guarantee coverage or a favorable recommendation. Sponsored arrangements must be clearly identified and do not purchase an editorial verdict.

Destinations, marinas and tourism partners

Include official dockage or launch information, seasonal dates, fuel status, current contacts and photography rights. The strongest pitches help boaters make a real planning decision rather than repeat a general tourism brochure. Local captains, harbormasters, historians and conservation organizations are especially useful sources.

Photography

When offering images, identify the photographer, location, capture date and the license or permission available for publication. Nautical Dream does not knowingly publish photographs without a traceable source and usable rights. See Privacy & Disclosure for the current image-credit policy.

Response expectations

Not every pitch receives a reply. Specific corrections and genuinely useful local information are prioritized. Attachments should be described before sending, and unsolicited executable files will not be opened.
